Our Guarantee: Transparent Cost & Paywall Auditing

In commercial software directories, apps often advertise themselves as "Free" because the APK download itself costs ₹0, only for students to discover during exam revision that core features (such as scheduled app blocking or website limits) require an expensive monthly subscription.

ReflectHub's Strict Editorial Rule: Any tool that requires payment for essential blocking, scheduling, or analytics features is classified as Freemium, and the exact paywalled tier is documented in the table above. Only tools with 100% unrestricted core capability are labeled Free.
