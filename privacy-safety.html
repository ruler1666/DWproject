1. Android Permissions Demystified

To intercept apps or enforce blocking, Android wellness tools ask for elevated system privileges. Knowing which permissions are legitimate — and which pose risks — protects your academic data.

✓ Low Risk
Usage Access (Android)

Allows an app to see which apps you've opened and for how long — but not what you typed or who you messaged. This is how StayFree and Digital Wellbeing count your screen time. Safe to grant to any reputable tool.

⚠ Medium Risk
Accessibility Service

Allows apps to monitor and interact with what's on-screen, intercept taps, and overlay UI elements. Necessary for blocking apps like StayFree to enforce limits. Only grant to open-source apps with published code — never to unknown apps.

! High Risk — Avoid
Device Admin / MDM

Gives an app full control to remotely wipe your device, enforce screen locks, or disable features. Legitimate wellness apps never need Device Admin. If an app requests this, uninstall it immediately.

3. Keeping Your Accounts Safe with Bitwarden

Reusing simple passwords across your college email, banking, and social apps is the most common way students get hacked. Bitwarden solves this with zero-knowledge AES-256 encryption — your master password never leaves your device.
